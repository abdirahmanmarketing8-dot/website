This is Ragn-Sells

Ragn-Sells is a privately held corporate group, operating companies in four countries. Since 1966, we've been involved in waste management, environmental services and recycling.

→

Trading in raw materials

Ragn-Sells Recyclables is today responsible for material sales and trading of six different material groups – paper, plastics, scrap and metals, recycled wood and wood chips, waste fuel, and tyres for energy production.

Latest Press Releases/News

Visit our newsroom

Madeleine Ljunggren appointed new Group CEO of Ragn-Sells

The Board of the Ragn-Sells Group has appointed Madeleine Ljunggren as the new Group CEO. She comes from the role of CEO of Ragn-Sells Treatment & Detox and was previously the Group's Chief Financial Officer. Madeleine Ljunggren succeeds Lars Lindén, who has retired.

"Resources should compete on quality not origin"

How can Europe strengthen resilience, competitiveness and food security using resources that already exist within its own borders? At a Brussels roundtable hosted by EU Commissioner Jessika Roswall, Pär Larshans (Ragn-Sells) shared his perspectives.

Jonas Hagman appointed new CEO of EasyMining

Jonas Hagman has been appointed as the new CEO of Ragn-Sells' innovation company EasyMining. Jonas joins from a long career within Perstorp and succeeds Jan Svärd, who, after 10 years as CEO, will take on a role as Senior Advisor.

Ragn-Sells launches digital training course aims to change view on waste

Ragn-Sells is now launching its award-winning e-learning What a waste! Think Circular to the general public.
