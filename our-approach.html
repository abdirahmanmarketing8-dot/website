Our approach to sustainability

Ragn-Sells' sustainability strategy guides our work towards sustainability and the transitioning to a circular society.

The overall goal for Ragn-Sells' sustainability strategy is:

  • our commitment to have net zero emissions by 2040
  • and to drive the transition to a society based on circular principles in a low carbon economy.

To fulfill this, we will counter climate emissions, biodiversity loss, and water scarcity, and pledge to:

  • Maintain and strengthen our current efforts to source raw materials from recycled resources and bring them safely back to the market.
  • Introduce more circular solutions to avoid climate emissions (CO2e) and prevent overshooting the planetary boundaries that otherwise would have been caused by others.
  • Reduce CO2e emissions to net zero by 2040.

Our sustainability strategy is based on our vision to be living proof that caring for the earth and business go hand in hand. We want to lead the transformation towards a circular society that cares for the environment, counters climate change, and helps communities prosper.

We also contribute to the transition into a circular economy, where waste is turned into resources to secure access to raw materials in society. This helps us counter climate change by decreasing CO2e emissions from the extraction of virgin materials. Since our core business is based on the idea of being sustainable, we consider our business strategy to be an integral part of our sustainability strategy. It gives us momentum in all sustainability work and consists of five areas that reinforce each other, helps us reach our sustainability goals, and creates competitive advantage.

Through our sustainability strategy, we strive towards integrating sustainability in all aspects of our business:

Value creation & innovation

We work with all stakeholders to develop innovative solutions and services that create value for the environment, society, and economy at large, as well as for Ragn-Sells as a business.

Climate and Environment

Our circular business model is designed to minimise negative environmental impact and reach our ambition of being climate positive by 2030. Find out more.

People and Culture

Our employees are at the heart of our business. Building a safe, inclusive, and visionary culture is a prerequisite in our ambition to transform society into a circular economy.

→

Responsible relations

Partnerships and cooperation are key to transform our linear economy towards a circular society.

Sustainability goals
