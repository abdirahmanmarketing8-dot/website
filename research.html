Research for the future

A growing population and increasing consumption levels put new demands on us as a recycling company. To meet these future challenges, we must stay a step ahead. This is why research and development is such an important part of our operations.

Our challenge is to develop, connect and utilise the knowledge needed to improve living conditions without undermining the conditions of life on earth. As conditions change and environmental goals are sharpened, we strive to be an organisation that can deliver good examples that contribute to a more sustainable society. A prioritised objective within all our areas of research is to find more ways to make resources out of waste, and through this move toward a circular economy.

Ongoing R&D projects

For Ragn-Sells, a prioritised objective within all our areas of research is to find more ways to make resources out of waste, and through this move toward a circular economy.
